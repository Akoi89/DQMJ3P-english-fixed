# What this build fixes, in detail

This is every change this build makes compared with the Joker 3 Translation Team's 2021-05-30 translation, grouped by what you'd notice on screen rather than by when I fixed it. `RELEASE_NOTES.md` has the version history, `CODE_PATCH_NOTES.md` has the executable changes for anyone porting them, and `BUILD_NOTES.md` has the checks.

## Crashes and stuck prompts

- **The font crash at The Core.** The original patch's broken font crashed the game for some players at the cutscene after the Break Archdemon boss. Both CIAs carry a corrected font from Lurpigi's Italian project, which matches the Japanese font layout and loses no glyph, and every character the game's text uses now exists in both fonts. The chest and transfer crashes reported in 2021 were already fixed in the 2021-05-30 build this starts from.
- **Yes/no prompts that closed before you could answer.** Five prompts had lost the code that shows the choice, among them the Rank 2 and 3 star Speed Road prompts (the second conduit in the Incarnus realm), which closed on their own and locked you out of the later races. They wait for your answer now, and a few system lines that had the wrong window style match the Japanese too.

## Text that said the wrong thing

The first full pass compared 12,563 translated strings with the Japanese for meaning and corrected 1,573. Later passes read the short interface text, read every line the first pass had passed a second time, checked every quest and hint line, and read the story and event lines again.

- **Stories told backwards or invented.** The Liquid Metal King's bestiary entry had slimes forming the king, where the Japanese has the king's drops becoming slimes. A monster was given its power "by demons in exchange for blood sacrifice" where the Japanese says it trained hard. The hero's identity reveal was told backwards, a boss's altar of sacrifice had become kneeling before a "new master" the Japanese never mentions, and a soldier in the bestiary betrayed his king where the king betrayed him.
- **Instructions and hints that sent you the wrong way.** A check of every quest, hint, tutorial, signpost, shop and NPC guidance line found 125 that said something the Japanese doesn't: a place, a direction, a container, a quantity or a condition. The other passes found more like them. A door that needs all three of Bundold's minions' keys had turned into three doors needing one each, a village guide dropped the instruction to find the Prison Key, Ace never said to go rescue the King from Glutton's Keep, and the two mine puzzle hints now say clockwise and counterclockwise.
- **Descriptions with the wrong effect.** Sizz spells dealt "Fire" damage where the Japanese says Light, a regeneration spell was described as an instant full heal, a trait that gives strong Ice resistance was said to increase Ice damage, nineteen counter traits had the attacker and target swapped, the ability Party Change swaps the opponent's party and not yours, and 54 trophy conditions were one short ("more than 100" where the Japanese means 100 or more).
- **Menus that said the opposite of what they do.** The Gold Bank's Deposit and Pocket labels were swapped, two SD card warnings said not to turn the system on where they mean off, a yes/no prompt that gives your books away read as if you'd get them back, a StreetPass warning said saving makes your monsters vanish where they vanish if you don't save, the status "cannot be revived" read "Cant Breath", and the Masters GP explainer warned how experienced the other masters are where the Japanese tells beginners not to worry.
- **Names and numbers in the wrong spot.** The game fills names and numbers into a sentence in a fixed order, and some lines had them the wrong way round: a Colour Fondude trader asked for "Red Gem of your 4", a reward read "received Incarnus' Stone 1", three pedestal devices promised their power to the monster and asked you to bring the Light Orb (the six pedestal texts are one template now, and fit their box), and a MAP Disc quest reward read "found 1000 xGold." It reads "received 1000 Gold." now.
- **Your partner's reports.** 28 lines had the partner saying you scouted, failed or found something when the partner was the one who did.
- **Who is who.** The King called Lucia "he" twice, Tiko and Kukuri were called "he" in places though the game's own monster pages call them both girls, and the enemy Mother was "mom".

## Spelling, and one name for one thing

Every item, skill, trait and action description, monster trivia entry, title description and menu help string (3,570) was proofread, with 301 slips fixed; every NPC, cutscene, quest and shop line (5,697), with 595 fixed; every menu, tutorial, battle, race and diary string (1,614), with 122 fixed; and the Helpful Tips, with 36 slips fixed, the three event names written without an apostrophe (Riders Cup, Masters GP, Challengers GP) and the Fondude family spelled the way the bestiary spells it. Guard and break traits are told apart, and the Crack ranks follow the same ladder as every other family. Typos in names are fixed too: "Sramp of Blastguard", "Stubborness", "Amulet of Clensing", "Shepard's Book", "Pusugon the Monsterous", "Heavens Thunder" without its apostrophe, and "A Dark, Cold, Place..." with its stray comma.

- **Monster names match the series.** Most monster names that didn't match the series were changed to match, taken from this game's bestiary list on the Dragon Quest Wiki and from dragon-quest.org and checked against the Japanese the game stores; monsters on neither source keep their 2021 names. Both sites are fan-run, so this isn't a claim that every name is an official Square Enix one. The monster called Great Empyrea is Great Godbird, as in Joker 2, and Terrorceratops and Tyrannoceratops, which the patch had swapped, have their official names. Two Joker 2 Professional monsters use that game's fan translation, Baboodread and Beastly Baboorood. The doll called "Cavorting Column" is Pocus Poppet, and the card-suit monsters have their suits back.
- **Monsters that aren't in the game.** Library descriptions called Ultra Slime, Nemeslime and Slider Girl "Slamen Rider", "Slamen Dark" and "Slamen Rider Girl", and Nelgel "Negel". They use the real names now.
- **Skill books, skill sets and traits.** Every skill book is named after the skill it teaches, as the skill table spells it ("Gravityguard SP Book" is "Gravity Ward SP Book"), and its help line names the same thing. Skill sets named after a monster use that monster's name, so a book no longer says one name on the cover and another underneath. Help lists that ran several skills into one line list one per line. 42 resistance traits that read "... Break" are "... Crafty", the series name, and traits that cast a spell use that spell's name ("Chance Buff"). In about fifty places an official English name existed and said something different, and it's used now: Gigabreak is Gigagash, the two regeneration spells are Reheal and Remidheal, and the rarer version of a trait says Rare instead of carrying a plus. Twenty-three help lists that said "Fullheal" for Moreheal say Moreheal, help texts that said "Blue" for Sublime Mist, "Pile Driver" for Swelling Strike, or "Attack Ward" and "Defense Ward" for Blunt Ward and Sap Ward use the battle menu's names, the mist skill sets match their spells (Black Mist Master), and the gravity spells are Kadonk, Kadonkle and Donkrumble; the last is coined, since that tier has no official English yet.
- **One name for fusion.** The feature had several English names (Unify, Merging, Union, Unity). It's fuse and fusion throughout now, with "Ride Fuse" kept for the command, and the battle Ride menu says "Nochorin Rides" and "Nochorin Dismounts" instead of "Ride with Nochorin."
- **Super Synthesis.** The upgraded fusion went by Super Synthesis, Super Fusion, Super Raw Fusion and Ultra Reincarnation Fusion, sometimes on the same screen. It's Super Synthesis everywhere now, and its upgrade is Super Synthesis+.
- **Hymn of Healing.** The healing flute was "Requiem of Restoration", too long for the battle status list and easy to mix up with Dire Dirge, the flute that really is a requiem. It's Hymn of Healing in the ability list, the status list, the battle log and every skill list.
- **Species is not family.** Monsters sort into nine families and many species. The status screen, the Reactor, the Library and the fusion panel mixed the two up, including a rule screen that said it restricts entry by species when it restricts by family. Sixteen labels now say whichever one the Japanese says.
- **Places and things with more than one name.** The "Phantom Gate" is the Demon Realm Gate, Madame Rummy had "Madam Deodora" over her dialogue box, and "Point Zero" is Ground Zero. The setting is "Break World" everywhere, SPD is AGI, "Technology Reflect" is ART Reflect, "Wisdom Ward" is Dumb Ward, JOKER is Wildcard, the battle tactic has one name on its menu and its own panel (it was "Psyche Up" on one and "Tension Gather" on the other), "Max HP" and "Max MP" are written the same way in the status list and item help as in the battle log, "Memorable Match-Ups" is Battle Memories, "bookstore" is the Book Shop, "Frame of Mind" is Fighting Spirit, Present Code, G-Cup, Network Coins and Wi-Fi Square are settled, and SpotPass and StreetPass are spelled Nintendo's way. Names the dialogue spelled two ways match the tables (Nochoro, Tiko, Lenate, Theresa, Mt. Elpis, Don Mole, Darkiron Bastille and more), and the Bastille's remodeling machine, named nine ways, has the two names the Japanese gives it.
- **One wording for one line.** Where the same Japanese line had ended up with two different English wordings and nobody was speaking to excuse the difference, it reads one way now: 168 short interface strings, monster names and system lines, plus 28 lines of narration and examine text.

## Text that didn't fit its box

The game doesn't word wrap and doesn't shrink text. A line wider than its box is cut in the middle of a word, and a line past the bottom of a box is drawn outside it, over whatever is underneath. Every string is now measured against the box that draws it.

- **Lines broken between words.** Every dialogue page and description is re-broken between words at its box, and the hand line breaks the original update files had lost are back.
- **Your name counted in.** A line that prints your name used to be measured as though the name were empty, so it ran past the box and the overflow went onto a third line the window never draws. Lines are measured with the name at its full 11 letters now.
- **Descriptions, warnings and speeches.** 49 Library descriptions didn't fit, 37 of them running to a fifth line drawn over the row below. 13 skill and trait descriptions were wider than their box, the new-game warning, a corrupted-data warning and a reactor readout were half again wider than the screen, all seven Fondude rescue speeches were cut mid-word, and six dialogue pages ran below the message window.
- **Page layout.** 64 lines left a stray space or a blank line at the top of the next page, and 26 pages ran to a third line because a short line had been broken by hand. All follow the Japanese page shape now. Eighteen field lines had the wrong speaker marking at their start and match the Japanese now.
- **Squeezed labels.** 135 labels had letter spacing tuned for the wide Japanese font, which ran English letters into each other ("Iems"). It's normal spacing now.
- **Tables, tips and counters.** Resistance values, sort-mode headers and family rows that overprinted or clipped are fixed. The 89 Helpful Tip titles dropped their category prefix to fit the 20 character title limit; 58 had been cut mid-word. The Library's Speed Road totals read "880Poi" with "nt" on the next line; they use "pt" like the race screen now.
- **Names glued to the next word.** A handful of lines printed a name straight into the next word ("Please give Jokesmanthe final test", "I will grant youGold Ring."); they have their space back. The roaming boss prompts ask "Challenge (name) to a fight?".
- **Item descriptions in the Library.** The Library's item box breaks a line partway through a word ("shines bri" / "lliantly with"), and 28 item descriptions had a line too wide for it. Their words are re-broken to fit now, with nothing reworded.
- **Lines that print an item or your name.** Four lines only ran past the dialogue box once the game dropped a long item name or your name into them: Ace handing over two items for a fusion, Kandata's apology, a scroll-pair quest and a ruin key starting to glow. Each breaks between words now: three where the Japanese breaks, and Ace's line the way its twin for Hogan already did. The Collab battle limit notice was one long line, far wider than any other menu message; it's two lines now, like the Japanese.
- **The fusion guide's menu.** Its last option, "Ultra Reincarnation Fusion Revision", was wider than the row it sits in. The options say "Super Synthesis" and "Super Synthesis+" now, the names the pages they open already use.
- **Help and tutorial lines.** A StreetPass help line, a disc keyword help line, the shortcut help, a Slime race tutorial line, the battle speed tutorial and two fusion screen lines were wider than anything the Japanese puts in those windows. Their words are re-broken to fit, with nothing reworded.
- **A letter too wide.** A few item and trait names were just wider than their box, so the last letter could be lost. They're shorter now.

## Names cut short

- **Wild monsters with no name in battle.** A species name longer than 11 characters was left out entirely, so 438 of the 880 species had no battle name, only the "A" or "B" that marks one of a pair. Long names are cut now ("Halberdsaur", or "Halberdsa A" and "Halberdsa B" for a pair), and the keyboard that names a monster you scout offers the whole species name instead of its first two letters. Reported by are-gar on GitHub.
- **Item names.** Item lists, the item info window and the equip lists cut names at 14 characters ("Strong Medicin"), and the reactor's analyze panes cut them at 10. They show in full now.
- **Monster names.** The Library's monster lists cut species names at 10 ("Metal Slim") and the Manage Monsters header cut a monster's own name at 10; both show in full now. The rename keyboard took 8 characters while a default name holds 11, so pressing OK on a long name cut it to 8; it takes 11 now. A fused monster's name read "合体Qu", with a Japanese prefix in front; it's the name alone now.
- **Skill-set names.** The status screen's Skill panel, the Library's Basic Info header and the Monsters list under Library > Skill cut names at 14 ("Wisdom Boost E"). They show in full now.
- **Ability names.** The Teaches list, Library > Abilities, a monster's skill pages on the status and battle Swap screens, the field ability menu, the Ride Fuse screen, the Allocate Skill Points Info window and the Set AI list cut names at 18 ("Giga Essence Extra", "Combustive Rending"). The skill-point hint under the counter cut them at 10 ("Venom Brea"). All show in full now. Two StreetPass match-up screens I never managed to reach are unchanged.
- **Player titles.** 54 titles are shortened to the 14 characters the game draws, and nine more are corrected. "Legendary Scout" showed as "Legendary Scou" and is "Legend Master" now.
- **Labels.** The race screen showed "Hors" and "Accel" for Horsepower and Acceleration. Nine labels that were too wide read Pwr, Acc, Plan, Speed, Sp., Rwd, Trait, W/L and "# Traits" now, and the Wi-Fi ranking's "Score" is W/L because the Japanese means a win and loss record. Three map place names are still too wide and are left alone on purpose, since shortening them would contradict the same names elsewhere.

## Japanese that was never translated

- **167 untranslated strings** are translated.
- **101 UI textures and 60 layout text panes** are English, along with the boot notice and the home screen title.
- **The StreetPass profile's default comment** was the Japanese よろしくおねがいします! and is "Hello there!" now.
- **Japanese punctuation.** 12 strings with literal Japanese punctuation use English punctuation, and 53 strings with a wide Japanese space have a normal one.
- **The crystal icon**, which the 2021 patch had swapped for a character no font has, is back in the three strings that show it.

## Characters that all sounded the same

The Japanese gives its characters their own way of talking, and the 2021 English mostly wrote everyone the same. The voices are back without changing what anyone says.

- **Who talks how.** Nochoros end a sentence with "cho", once per page and never on a yes/no question, and your Nochorin partner's StreetPass greetings do too. Chomach says "Whoosh!" and is always in a hurry. The Nochoro Queen is a grand lady ("good sir", "ever so") instead of the Southern belle a handful of 2021 lines made her. Toto is a gruff old boss who calls you "lad", Azamook is politely, unctuously evil ("Ho ho ho!"), the King, Itaburu and Madullajah drop contractions the way their Japanese does, Sancho talks like a robot butler, Lucia calls you "kid" and Hogan says "pal".
- **The swearing.** The 2021 English added swearing the Japanese doesn't have ("bastard" for a plain "you!", "damn" and worse). Those lines say "curse you", "scoundrel", "blast it" or "heck" now, matched to how strong the Japanese is.
- **The made-up laughs.** On about 25 lines someone laughed where the Japanese doesn't ("Gwahaha, welcome!" from the Dragonlord), and those laughs are gone. Where the Japanese is only a scoff, the English says "Hmph" instead of "Heh heh heh", and Toto's one real laugh is back on the page where he laughs.

## Smaller things

- 41 item names carried a stray space at the end from the 2021 patch, so a line that printed one put two spaces after it (`Fusing the Δ Chimaera's Heart  with the`). The space is gone.
- The name-entry keyboard opens on the Latin "Aa" tab instead of hiragana, and its kanji page, where the 2021 patch had left English words like "Round" and "Breath" on the keys, is restored.
- The title menu shows the version of each patch: the box above Continue reads "EN 2.14" from the update, and a line in the bottom corner reads "base EN 2.14" from the base. No corner line means the base patch didn't install (older builds of this patch show neither).
- The boss-fight forfeit button says "Give up" rather than "Defeat All", which read as an order to win the fight.
- A monster's Library entry with more fusion recipes than it shows says "etc." under them again, and bestiary habitats read "Fiery Volcano etc." instead of "as Fiery Volcano".
- The Red Colour Fondude's menu listed the Yellow trader's rewards and then handed over something else. It lists its own now.
- Ten one-shot battle reflects are marked "x1" so they read differently from the kind that lasts, and the partner's errands read "5 min" and "30 min".
- The StreetPass country list had Malta as "GreatLog" and the Tokai and Chugoku regions as "East Sea" and "China". They have their real names.
- The Full Charge event line says "You learned Full Charge, Ride Fuse and Attempt Fusion!" instead of "remembered how to ride your monsters!".
- Nochorin's moves on his Ally page use his battle orders' names, and the Nokturnus skill book's last ability no longer runs into "MASTERED" under the wrong name.
- A few Library descriptions ended with no full stop, ran "Blueeater" and "Redeater" together as single words, or said "Dermlin island".
- The Records page counts "Boxes", not "Box's", and the ride warning no longer says "You cannot ride the" in front of your monster's nickname.
