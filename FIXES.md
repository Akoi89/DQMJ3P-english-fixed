# What is fixed, one paragraph each

The full list. `RELEASE_NOTES.md` is the short version; `BUILD_NOTES.md` has the build-by-build record with the checks.

**The crash.** The original patch ships a broken font that crashes the game for some players at The Core, the cutscene after the Break Archdemon boss. Both CIAs carry the corrected font (from Lurpigi's Italian project), which matches the Japanese font layout and loses no glyph. On top of that, the four glyphs the Japanese Ver.1.3 update added to its fonts (three kanji and the crystal icon) are appended to both fonts, and the crystal icon is back in the three strings that show it; the 2021 patch had replaced it with a character no font has. Every character used by every string in the build now exists in both fonts. The chest and transfer crashes reported in 2021 were already fixed in the 2021-05-30 build, which is what this starts from.

**The text.** 167 untranslated strings are translated. Every dialogue page and description is re-broken between words at its box (the engine wraps by character). The corrupted name-entry keyboard is restored, and factual errors, wrong numbers and spelling are fixed. Names are made consistent with each other: skill book names match the skill they teach, guard and break traits are told apart, the Crack ranks follow the same ladder as every other family, and the card-suit monsters have their suits back. 54 player titles are shortened to the 14-character cap the game enforces, and nine more corrected. The hand line breaks that the original update files had lost are put back, so descriptions no longer split words at the box edge.

**The names, and the player's own (v2.5).** Every monster name that didn't match the name the series uses has been changed to match, 356 of them, taken from this game's own bestiary list on the Dragon Quest Wiki and from dragon-quest.org and checked against the Japanese the game stores. 391 more were left as they were because those sources agreed with them, and 113 monsters are on neither source and keep their 2021 names. Both sites are fan-run, so a few of the names they carry are fan inventions too; this is not a claim that every name is an official Square Enix one. Four had been crossed with a different monster: the doll called "Cavorting Column" is Pocus Poppet, and the name it had belongs to ミステリピラー. The 凶 forms written with a delta are "Evil Slime" and so on. 152 monsters have no series name to match and keep what the 2021 translation gave them. 42 resistance traits that read "... Break" are "... Crafty", which is the series name for them, while the "... Ward" traits were already right. The boss-fight forfeit button says "Give up" rather than "Defeat All", which read as an order to win the fight. And a dialogue line that carries the player's name is measured with the name in it: it used to be measured as though the name were empty, so the line ran past the box, the overflow was pushed onto a third line, and since the window holds two lines that text was never drawn at all.

**The graphics.** 101 UI textures and 60 layout text panes that the original patch left in Japanese are English, plus the boot notice and the home screen title.

**The tables.** The resistance values, sort-mode headers and family rows that overprinted, clipped or mistranslated are fixed.

**The squeezed labels.** 135 layout text panes (the AGI and INT stat labels, the bestiary's "Items" pill, map place names, StreetPass counters) carried a minus 2 px letter spacing tuned for the wide Japanese font. On the English font it ran narrow letters into each other ("Iems"). The spacing is zero now.

**The help layer.** Every item, skill, trait and action description, monster trivia entry, title description and menu help string (3,570 of them) was proofread. 301 spelling, agreement and wording slips are fixed, skill names in help lists are aligned with the skill table, and the family names in title descriptions are aligned with the monster table.

**The Helpful Tips.** The 89 tip titles lost their category prefix so they fit the game's 20 character title limit (58 were cut mid-word), eight were shortened further, and 36 slips in the tip bodies are fixed. The setting is "Break World" everywhere (twelve lines said "Broken World"), the three event names carry no apostrophe (Riders Cup, Masters GP, Challengers GP), and the Fondude family is spelled the way the bestiary spells it.

**The map marker.** The Silent Meadows navi map showed "NOW_PRINTING" on its destination marker. That was this rebuild's own doing: the layout pane carries a hidden message ID after its text, and an earlier text-pane edit dropped it. The ID is back, the pane reads "Wood Park", and the writer no longer drops such IDs.

**The prompts.** Every line's control codes were compared with the Japanese line's. Five yes/no prompts had lost their prompt code, among them the Rank ★★ and ★★★ Speed Road prompts (the second conduit in the Incarnus realm, where the yes/no box never appeared), which closed before a choice could be made and locked players out of the later races. One had the code in the wrong place, one had a spurious one, and a few system lines carried the wrong window style. All mirror the Japanese now.

**The keyboard.** The name-entry keyboard opens on the Latin "Aa" tab instead of hiragana. This is a three-word patch to the update's executable.

**Item names.** Every item list, the item info window and the equip lists cut item names at 14 characters ("Strong Medicin"); 584 of the 1,079 names are longer. Three 16-character buffers in the update's executable are raised to 32 (thirteen words changed), so names draw in full. The reactor's two analyze panes cut them at 10 the same way; that buffer is raised too (four more words).

**Monster names.** The Library's monster lists cut species names at 10 characters ("Metal Slim"; 495 of the 1,024 names are longer) because one row builder formats each name into a 16-character buffer behind a five-unit family icon. That buffer is now 38 characters (32 visible; the longest name is 24). The Manage Monsters header cut the monster's own name at 10 through a 12-character buffer, now 32. The rename keyboard took 8 characters while a default name holds 11, so opening Change Name on a monster with a long name and pressing OK cut it to 8. The keyboard now takes 11, its four handlers copy 11, and the two copies that carry a parent's name into a synthesized monster's record keep 11 instead of 10. Twenty-seven words in the update's executable, found with a live debugger and each seen on screen.

**Skill books, habitats and one place name (v1.2).** Thirty skill books had kept the 2021 stem ("Gravityguard SP Book", "Confuguard book") next to siblings already renamed ("Gravity Ward+ Book"): they now carry the skill table's name plus "Book", the SP and EX ranks included ("Gravity Ward SP Book", "Quake & Gravity Ward EX Book"). Five bestiary habitats read "as Fiery Volcano", "as Map" and so on, a broken rendering of the Japanese "Fiery Volcano and others"; they read "Fiery Volcano etc." and "World Map etc." The place the game calls Ground Zero was "Point Zero" in five lines (the diary, the Zoom list, the wormhole prompt); all say Ground Zero. And the trait "Heavens Thunder" has its apostrophe.

**Three more 14-character panels (v1.3).** The status screen's Skill panel cut skill-set names at 14 ("Wisdom Boost E" for "Wisdom Boost EX"), and so did the Library's Basic Info header ("Metal Pearl Sl") and the Monsters list under Library > Skill. Two sibling builders in the executable format an icon and a name into a 16-character buffer, which leaves exactly 14 characters of the name; that one shared shape is why three unrelated screens cut at the same place. Both buffers are now 32 characters, with their stack frames grown to match. Thirteen words, found with a live debugger and each panel checked on screen.

**Fifteen more skill books, and the Sublime mist (v1.3).** Round 25 missed some books whose old names had no "guard" in them; each is now named after the skill it teaches, as the skill table spells it. Three stat Ward EX books read "Slow Ward EX Book", "Wisdom Ward EX Book" and "MP Drain Ward EX Book" (they were "SPD", "INT" and "MP Ward EX Book"). The five Mist SP books read "Black / Red / White / Netherworld / Sublime Mister SP Book", matching their base books; the skill itself was "Black Mister+", the only mist skill written with a plus, and is now "Black Mister SP". The six fourth-rank stat books, "HP Up SP Book" and its siblings, teach "HP Boost EX" and so on, but their names and help lines named the third rank, which other books teach; they now read "HP Boost EX Book" to "Wisdom Boost EX Book", and their help lines say the same. The spell 青天の霧 is "Sublime Mist" in the game's own tables, yet seven help texts and one of its two trait labels still said "Blue"; they all say Sublime now. And "Shepard's Book" is "Shepherd's Book".

**Help lines that named the wrong thing (v1.4).** A check of every skill book's help text against the skill table found sixteen more mismatches. The seven Dai collab books listed several skills to a line ("Kaclang Bang-flurry Zam Ward+ Gravity Ward+"), because the 2021 text was one flat line; they now list one per line like every other book, and the Vearn, the Beholder book calls パイルハンマー "Swelling Strike", as the battle menu does. Four help lines still said "Attack Ward" and "Defense Ward" for Blunt Ward and Sap Ward. The recovery SP skills had three names each ("Cure-All+", "Mass Recovery SP Book", "Skill: Cure-All SP"); they are "Cure-All SP", "Purifier SP" and "Mage's Aid SP" on skill, book and help line alike. ＪＯＫＥＲ's book and help line say "Wildcard", its name everywhere else. "Pusugon the Monsterous" is spelled "Monstrous", and its book, "Mysterious Pusugon's Book" (怪獣 is "monster"), is "Pusugon the Monstrous Book".

**The rest of those help lists (v1.5).** The seven Dai collab skill sets have their own help text, the list synthesis shows under Info when you pick skills to pass on, and each was still one flat line; they now list one skill per line, like the books. "Pile Driver" still named パイルハンマー in nine more help texts, and "Attack Ward+" and "Defense Ward+" still named the ranked Wards in 34 help-list lines; they say "Swelling Strike", "Blunt Ward+" and "Sap Ward+", the names the battle menu and the skill table use.

**Moreheal, and a column that cut at 18 (v1.6).** Twenty-three help lists, ten books and thirteen skill sets, said "Fullheal" where the Japanese names ベホイマ, the spell the battle menu calls "Moreheal"; "Fullheal" is ベホマ, the stronger one. They say "Moreheal" now. And a skill set's Teaches list, under Allocate Skill Points and Library > Skill, cut every action name at 18 characters ("Miracle of the Sta", "Giga Essence Extra"): the function that builds each row gave the name a 21-character buffer, two icons and 18 letters. The buffer is 32 characters now. Five words, found with a live debugger and checked on screen. Eleven names were affected; the longest, "Essence Extractor Dance", fits the column.

**The other screens that cut at 18 (v1.7).** The Teaches column fixed in v1.6 was one of fourteen places in the executable that build an action name with its icons into a 21-character buffer. A debugger hunt through the menus, a battle, the field ability menu and the Library found five more that players see: Library > Abilities cut "Combustive Rending Arts" to "Combustive Rending", and a monster's skill pages, on the status screen and the battle Swap screen, cut "Giga Essence Extractor" and "Miracle of the Stars" the way the Teaches column did. The field ability menu's list, its Info header and the row above Use and Cancel had the same buffer. All five hold 32 characters now, 21 words in all. The battle ability list, its Info window and the action banner never cut. Eight more places with the same buffer were not reached on any screen checked, and are unchanged.

**The skill-point hint, and two small slips (v1.8).** In Allocate Skill Points, the line under the counter ("Learns:" or "20 SP unlocks:") cut every ability name at 10 letters: "Venom Brea", "Hatchet Ma", "Disruptive". Its name was built into the same 21-character buffer as the other cut screens, but with coloured icons whose colour codes used up 10 of the 21. The buffer holds 40 characters now; five words, found with the debugger and checked on screen. The ability 闇獄凍滅斬 is "A Dark, Cold Place..." instead of "A Dark, Cold, Place...", keeping the 2021 phrase without the comma before the noun, and the accessory 破毒のおまもり is "Amulet of Cleansing" instead of "Amulet of Clensing".

**Ride Fuse, one name for it, and the last cut names (v1.9).** The Ride Fuse screen, Fusion Info, cut ability names at 18 on its Unify Ability line and its ability pages ("Giga Essence Extra", "A Dark, Cold Place"), and the Info window in Allocate Skill Points had the same limit; they show whole names now, 16 more words in the executable. A fused monster's name read "合体Qu", a Japanese prefix built into the executable; it is the name alone now, with room for all 11 letters. The feature itself, 合体 in Japanese, had five English names: "Ride Fuse" for the command, "Fusion Info" for its screen, "Unify" on that screen's labels and in the AI settings, "Merging" in the help titles, "Union" and "Unity" once each. It is fuse and fusion throughout now, with "Ride Fuse" kept for the command: "Fusion Ability", "Fusion Traits", "Fused From", "AI Fusion On", "Stay Fused Next Battle" and the rest, the six tutorial pages, the help pages and three event lines (the Full Charge monument hint, and the Scout-Q #2 message, which now names the trait "Fusion Expert"). In the battle Ride menu, "Ride with Nochorin." and "Dismount with Nochorin." say "Nochorin Rides" and "Nochorin Dismounts", which is what they do (ノチョリンが乗る / 降りる). Of the fourteen places that built an action name into the short buffer, only two StreetPass match-up screens were never reached; they are unchanged. Three smaller slips from the same pass: the StreetPass profile's default comment was the Japanese よろしくおねがいします!, built into the executable, and is "Hello there!" now; the Liquid Metal King's bestiary entry had its slimes forming the king, where the Japanese has the king's drops becoming slimes, and it follows the Japanese now; and the event line that sums up Full Charge says "You learned Full Charge, Ride Fuse and Attempt Fusion!" instead of "remembered how to ride your monsters!".

**The meaning pass (v2.0).** Earlier builds proofread spelling and wording; this one checks what each line says. Every translated string that differs from the Japanese (12,563: 737 bestiary entries, 2,725 other descriptions, 6,367 dialogue lines, 2,734 menu, tips, battle, diary and trophy strings) was compared with the Japanese for meaning, and each possible error was judged again and drafted with the scene or the list around it in view. 1,573 are corrected, plus 85 identical copies. In the bestiary (251): stories told backwards (the Liquid Metal King, a dragon sealed by the goddess of light that "used her power to seal" the World Tree), invented backstories (a monster given its power "by demons in exchange for blood sacrifice" where the Japanese says it trained hard), the five contaminated monsters whose minds turned vicious described as "broken". In the descriptions (211): Sizz spells dealing "Fire" damage (the Japanese says Light), a regeneration spell described as an instant full heal, a trait that gives strong Ice resistance described as increasing Ice damage, nineteen counter traits with the attacker and target swapped, a Wi-Fi save warning rewritten into a different warning. In the dialogue (695): Sancho's "Try not to forget" (the Japanese: it is fine to leave the past forgotten), the first tutorial telling you to scout a monster you are told to defeat, the hero's identity reveal told backwards in three lines, a warning sign telling you to free the prisoners, a boss who called himself "Estarkid". In the other text (416): prompts that said the opposite of what their button does, fusion messages that said traits would be "forgotten" when they are unlocked, the Gold Bank's Deposit and Pocket labels swapped, two SD card warnings that told you not to turn the handheld on where they mean off, a message that said a fall costs HP and gold, and 54 trophy conditions one short ("more than 100" for 以上, "100 or more"). The names that run through all of it are settled once: 360 skill-tree and skill-book lines use the battle menu's ability and trait names; サウルスロード is Terrorceratops and バザックス Tyrannoceratops, their official names (the patch had them swapped); Gran Estark, not Grand Estark; 魔界 is the Demon Realm; the online currency is Network Coins; the disc keywords are "Land", "Sea" and "Sky", as the disc machine lists them; and item names match the item list (Sirloin Voucher, Sea Map Fragment, Incarnus' Stone). Every corrected line keeps its control codes and value slots in the order the game fills them, and fits its box, with the player's name counted at its full 11 letters.

**The meaning pass, leftovers (v2.1).** Four names and a handful of lines the v2.0 pass had set aside. 魔界の門, the gate to the Demon Realm, was the "Phantom Gate" (and once a "phantom door") on the map, in the habitat list and in the dialogue; it is the Demon Realm Gate now, and three lines that say only "the gate" or "the door to the Demon Realm" in the Japanese say that. Madame Rummy of Palaish Isle, her official name from Dragon Quest Monsters: Joker, had "Madam Deodora" over her dialogue box. The かしこさガード skill trees and their books were "Wisdom Ward" while the traits they teach are "Dumb Ward"; they are Dumb Ward now, like Blunt Ward and Slow Ward. Nochorin's moves on his Ally page use his battle orders' names ("Rabble Rouser a gogo!", "Wound healing a gogo!"). And six lines: a henchman of Yetas who doubts Toto's offer of peace was made to agree with it; マザー, the enemy Mother, was "mom"; a stray closing quote; one signpost that called 魔王の間 "Demon King's Room" where the others say "Djinn's Throne"; a ghost's Library record that had it "trapped" where the Japanese has it lingering by its own attachment; and the Nokturnus skill book, whose last ability ran into "MASTERED" under the wrong name (Giga Cross Break).

**The meaning pass, the UI and a second look (v2.2).** The v2.0 pass had not read the short UI text, so the 2,279 button, header, option, status, place, disc and trophy strings were compared with the Japanese as well; 83 are corrected, and 2 identical copies with them. Among them: the Masters GP explainer warned that the other masters are very experienced, where the Japanese says beginners are given an easier time, so join without worry; the ranking registration explainers left out that playing on a different save overwrites your data and that it is deleted after a long time without updates; the status 蘇生不可 (cannot be revived) was "Cant Breath"; the race option "Local Great Rider Race" is a race against the Special Masters; the partner order "Scout Mid-Rank" scouts monsters you do not own; the StreetPass country list had Malta as "GreatLog" and the Tokai and Chugoku regions as "East Sea" and "China". To see what the v2.0 pass had missed, 600 of the lines it passed were read again at random; 65 of them are corrected, with 9 identical lines elsewhere (a save copied "from" the SD card when it is copied to it, "You cannot cross water" for diving, a boss notice that left out that you need free storage to fight it, the Nochoro secret books that "increase the stock when sold" when you take them to the book store). The partner's reports were read again too: 28 lines had the partner saying you scouted, failed or found what the partner did. And names: ＪＯＫＥＲ is Wildcard in 9 more lines, すばやさ is AGI in 28 strings that said SPD, 体技反射 is "ART Reflect" everywhere (it was also "Technology Reflect"), the Traits Library trophies say Traits, Shikoku is spelled right.

**The second read (v2.2).** Then every line the v2.0 pass had passed (9,727) was read again, this time for real errors only: a wrong fact, condition, number or name, a reversed direction, a consequence left out. 261 are corrected (121 in the dialogue, 34 descriptions, 106 menu, tips, battle and trophy strings), 13 of them serious. Among them: the ability Party Change swaps the opponent's party, not yours; a StreetPass warning said saving makes your monsters vanish, where they vanish if you do not save them; last week's tickets "collected" were taken back; a trait said it counters paralyzed enemies, where a paralyzed monster may paralyze the ones that hit it; a team rule said your monsters must be of one family, where the Japanese only allows it; a puzzle hint said "to the right" for clockwise; a yes/no prompt that gives your books away read as if you would get them back; a soldier in the bestiary betrayed his king where the king betrayed him. Almost all of these were in the 2021 translation; one was ours (a bat named "Corpsebat" in a round 37 correction, where the Japanese names ほねこうもり).

**Loose ends (v2.2).** The StreetPass list of saved battles (思い出の戦い) is "Battle Memories" everywhere; its menu said "Memorable Match-Ups", a name too long for eight of the prompts that point to it. 本屋 is "the Book Shop", as the menu names it, in the 19 item and shop lines that said "bookstore", "book store" or "Book Store" or left out the article. The Helpful Tip about the field aura says "Fighting Spirit", as the field and the Reactor do (it said "Frame of Mind"). The line after "the King of Power and the King of Wisdom" called them Gods, and had Wildcard "with another name" where the Japanese says Wildcard is the other name of the Incarnus who once saved the island; one battle message, one summon line and the Scout-Q #8 hint say the same thing the same way as their siblings.

**The last of the meaning pass, and one name for one thing (v2.3).** The meaning pass, which reads what a line says against the Japanese rather than only how it reads in English, is finished: every line it ever flagged has now been judged, and 192 more corrections come out of its last two rounds. Twelve of those had misled a player about the story or about what to do next. A boss's altar of sacrifice had become kneeling before a "new master" the Japanese never mentions; a door that needs all three of Bundold's minions' keys had turned into three separate doors each needing one; a village guide had dropped the instruction to go and find the Prison Key. The rest soften, drop or invent a smaller fact, in dialogue, trait and item descriptions and monster trivia. The rest of the release is consistency. Where the same Japanese line had ended up with two different English wordings and nobody was speaking to excuse the difference, it now reads one way: 168 short interface strings, monster names and system lines are settled, along with 28 lines of narration and examine text that had been treated as though a character were speaking them. The battle tactic no longer reads "Psyche Up" on the menu and "Tension Gather" on its own panel. Ten one-shot battle reflects are marked "x1" so they read differently from the kind that lasts. The "Max HP" and "Max MP" casing settled for the battle log now covers the status list and the item help it had missed, so the two screens agree about the same effect. Eighteen field lines have their opening speaker code corrected to match the Japanese, 53 strings carrying an oversized ideographic space where a normal space belongs are narrowed, and 12 strings of literal Japanese punctuation, a corner bracket and an ideographic full stop among them, are swapped for their English equivalents.

**A species is not a family (v2.3).** Dragon Quest Monsters sorts every monster into one of nine families, Slime, Dragon, Beast, Nature, Demon, Undead, Material, Break and ???, and each family holds many species. Japanese uses two different words for those two ideas, and the English had them tangled: the status screen and the Reactor's analysis called a monster's species its "Type", the Library heading and the fusion panel called it a "Family", and the community rules screen said it would restrict entry by "species" when it restricts by family. One line gives the game away: the milestone for recruiting monsters says "over 20", which is an ordinary target for species and impossible for families, since there are nine. Sixteen labels now say species where the Japanese says species and family where it says family. The one rule text that already had both right is untouched, and so are the labels whose Japanese is a third word again and correctly read "Type".

**Two counters (v2.3).** The Library's Speed Road totals used to read "880Poi" with "nt" dropped onto the next line, because the game writes its own number and then our label straight after it, with nothing in between, and the word "Point" was too wide for the space. The game already had the answer: the race screen writes the same counter as "pt", and so does the Japanese there, so the other two now match it. The Records page also counted "Stealth Box's Found", which is now "Boxes". And the partner's scouting menu, which listed its errands as "5min" and "30min", now puts a space in: "5 min", "30 min", the way the game already writes "5 Mon." elsewhere. Two lines had lost the space in front of a name the game fills in, so a reward read "I will grant youGold Ring." and a disc prompt read "UseSunken Ruins Lv3 and freely roam"; both have their space back.

**The quest and hint text, checked against the Japanese.** After the Shiny Sap hint turned out to point at the wrong kind of chest, every quest instruction, hint, tutorial, signpost, shop and NPC guidance line (1,007 of them) was read beside its Japanese with one question: is it true? 125 lines said something the Japanese does not (a place, a direction, a container, a quantity, a condition, or what the player is told to do) and now say what the Japanese says. Three names the dialogue used are now the names the tables use (Δ Slime, Don Mole, Darkiron Bastille), and the two mine puzzle hints say clockwise and counterclockwise.

**The dialogue.** Every NPC line, cutscene line, quest and shop line (5,697 strings) was proofread the same way. 595 spelling, agreement, wrong-word and stray-token slips are fixed, and the names the dialogue spelled two ways are aligned with the game's own tables (Nochoro, Tiko, Lenate, Theresa, Mt. Elpis, Undead Garden and a dozen more). Sixteen lines the 2021 text had garbled are put right from the Japanese, among them the cryogenic-sleep records, the boulder line in the crystal caves, the red and yellow mushrooms that both said pink, and the dying underling who now addresses his two bats instead of claiming to be one.

**The menus.** The last layer: every menu prompt, tutorial pop-up, battle event line, Speed Road and race string, reactor readout, diary entry and network screen (1,614 strings). 122 slips are fixed, and the terms the menus spelled several ways are settled (Present Code, G-Cup, Network Coins, Wi-Fi Square, and SpotPass and StreetPass spelled Nintendo's way everywhere, dialogue included). Ten lines are corrected from the Japanese, among them two Speed Road attacks labelled ranged that are close-range, three that said one slime drops where all of them do, and a garbled diary line about the ship remains.

**The page layout.** Every dialogue page was measured against the same page of the Japanese. In 64 lines the page-break code was wrapped in spaces or glued to the text where the Japanese breaks the line, which left a stray space or a blank line at the top of the next page. A few pages opened with an empty line, 26 pages ran to a third line because a short line had been broken by hand, and one line had two Japanese pages on one. All are back in the Japanese shape, with no wording changed. Five more pages whose English simply had too many words for two lines were shortened, and one prompt that was not a sentence was retranslated. The Bastille's remodeling machine, named nine ways in the 2021 text, has the two names the Japanese gives it; the Snapped family is named by its table names; and the chapter 7 hint, which said the opposite of the Japanese, is retranslated.
