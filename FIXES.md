# What is fixed, one paragraph each

The full list. `RELEASE_NOTES.md` is the short version; `BUILD_NOTES.md` has the build-by-build record with the checks.

**The crash.** The original patch ships a broken font that crashes the game for some players at The Core, the cutscene after the Break Archdemon boss. Both CIAs carry the corrected font (from Lurpigi's Italian project), which matches the Japanese font layout and loses no glyph. On top of that, the four glyphs the Japanese Ver.1.3 update added to its fonts (three kanji and the crystal icon) are appended to both fonts, and the crystal icon is back in the three strings that show it; the 2021 patch had replaced it with a character no font has. Every character used by every string in the build now exists in both fonts. The chest and transfer crashes reported in 2021 were already fixed in Eiz's 2021-05-30 build, which is what this starts from.

**The text.** 167 untranslated strings are translated. Every dialogue page and description is re-broken between words at its box (the engine wraps by character). The corrupted name-entry keyboard is restored, and factual errors, wrong numbers and spelling are fixed. Names are made consistent with each other: skill book names match the skill they teach, guard and break traits are told apart, the Crack ranks follow the same ladder as every other family, and the card-suit monsters have their suits back. 54 player titles are shortened to the 14-character cap the game enforces, and nine more corrected. The hand line breaks that the original update files had lost are put back, so descriptions no longer split words at the box edge.

**The graphics.** 101 UI textures and 60 layout text panes that the original patch left in Japanese are English, plus the boot notice and the home screen title.

**The tables.** The resistance values, sort-mode headers and family rows that overprinted, clipped or mistranslated are fixed.

**The squeezed labels.** 135 layout text panes (the AGI and INT stat labels, the bestiary's "Items" pill, map place names, StreetPass counters) carried a minus 2 px letter spacing tuned for the wide Japanese font. On the English font it ran narrow letters into each other ("Iems"). The spacing is zero now.

**The help layer.** Every item, skill, trait and action description, monster trivia entry, title description and menu help string (3,570 of them) was proofread. 301 spelling, agreement and wording slips are fixed, skill names in help lists are aligned with the skill table, and the family names in title descriptions are aligned with the monster table.

**The Helpful Tips.** The 89 tip titles lost their category prefix so they fit the game's 20 character title limit (58 were cut mid-word), eight were shortened further, and 36 slips in the tip bodies are fixed. The setting is "Break World" everywhere (twelve lines said "Broken World"), the three event names carry no apostrophe (Riders Cup, Masters GP, Challengers GP), and the Fondude family is spelled the way the bestiary spells it.

**The map marker.** The Silent Meadows navi map showed "NOW_PRINTING" on its destination marker. That was this rebuild's own doing: the layout pane carries a hidden message ID after its text, and an earlier text-pane edit dropped it. The ID is back, the pane reads "Wood Park", and the writer no longer drops such IDs.

**The prompts.** Every line's control codes were compared with the Japanese line's. Five yes/no prompts had lost their prompt code, among them the Rank ★★ and ★★★ Speed Road prompts (the second conduit in the Incarnus realm, where the yes/no box never appeared), which closed before a choice could be made and locked players out of the later races. One had the code in the wrong place, one had a spurious one, and a few system lines carried the wrong window style. All mirror the Japanese now.

**The keyboard.** The name-entry keyboard opens on the Latin "Aa" tab instead of hiragana. This is a three-word patch to the update's executable.

**Item names.** Every item list, the item info window and the equip lists cut item names at 14 characters ("Strong Medicin"); 584 of the 1,079 names are longer. Three 16-character buffers in the update's executable are raised to 32 (thirteen words changed), so names draw in full. The reactor's two analyze panes cut them at 10 the same way; that buffer is raised too (four more words).

**Monster names.** The Library's monster lists cut species names at 10 characters ("Metal Slim"; 495 of the 1,024 names are longer) because one row builder formats each name into a 16-character buffer behind a five-unit family icon. That buffer is now 38 characters (32 visible; the longest name is 24). The Manage Monsters header cut the monster's own name at 10 through a 12-character buffer, now 32. The rename keyboard took 8 characters while a default name holds 11, so opening Change Name on a monster with a long name and pressing OK cut it to 8. The keyboard now takes 11, its four handlers copy 11, and the two copies that carry a parent's name into a synthesized monster's record keep 11 instead of 10. Twenty-seven words in the update's executable, found with a live debugger and each seen on screen.

**Skill books, habitats and one place name (v1.2).** Thirty skill books had kept the 2021 stem ("Gravityguard SP Book", "Confuguard book") next to siblings already renamed ("Gravity Ward+ Book"): they now carry the skill table's name plus "Book", the SP and EX ranks included ("Gravity Ward SP Book", "Quake & Gravity Ward EX Book"). Five bestiary habitats read "as Fiery Volcano", "as Map" and so on, a broken rendering of the Japanese "Fiery Volcano and others"; they read "Fiery Volcano etc." and "World Map etc." The place the game calls Ground Zero was "Point Zero" in five lines (the diary, the Zoom list, the wormhole prompt); all say Ground Zero. And the trait "Heavens Thunder" has its apostrophe.

**Three more 14-character panels (v1.3).** The status screen's Skill panel cut skill-set names at 14 ("Wisdom Boost E" for "Wisdom Boost EX"), and so did the Library's Basic Info header ("Metal Pearl Sl") and the Monsters list under Library > Skill. Two sibling builders in the executable format an icon and a name into a 16-character buffer, which leaves exactly 14 characters of the name; that one shared shape is why three unrelated screens cut at the same place. Both buffers are now 32 characters, with their stack frames grown to match. Thirteen words, found with a live debugger and each panel checked on screen.

**Fifteen more skill books, and the Sublime mist (v1.3).** Round 25 missed some books whose old names had no "guard" in them; each is now named after the skill it teaches, as the skill table spells it. Three stat Ward EX books read "Slow Ward EX Book", "Wisdom Ward EX Book" and "MP Drain Ward EX Book" (they were "SPD", "INT" and "MP Ward EX Book"). The five Mist SP books read "Black / Red / White / Netherworld / Sublime Mister SP Book", matching their base books; the skill itself was "Black Mister+", the only mist skill written with a plus, and is now "Black Mister SP". The six fourth-rank stat books, "HP Up SP Book" and its siblings, teach "HP Boost EX" and so on, but their names and help lines named the third rank, which other books teach; they now read "HP Boost EX Book" to "Wisdom Boost EX Book", and their help lines say the same. The spell 青天の霧 is "Sublime Mist" in the game's own tables, yet seven help texts and one of its two trait labels still said "Blue"; they all say Sublime now. And "Shepard's Book" is "Shepherd's Book".

**Help lines that named the wrong thing (v1.4).** A check of every skill book's help text against the skill table found sixteen more mismatches. The seven Dai collab books listed several skills to a line ("Kaclang Bang-flurry Zam Ward+ Gravity Ward+"), because the 2021 text was one flat line; they now list one per line like every other book, and the Vearn, the Beholder book calls パイルハンマー "Swelling Strike", as the battle menu does. Four help lines still said "Attack Ward" and "Defense Ward" for Blunt Ward and Sap Ward. The recovery SP skills had three names each ("Cure-All+", "Mass Recovery SP Book", "Skill: Cure-All SP"); they are "Cure-All SP", "Purifier SP" and "Mage's Aid SP" on skill, book and help line alike. ＪＯＫＥＲ's book and help line say "Wildcard", its name everywhere else. "Pusugon the Monsterous" is spelled "Monstrous", and its book, "Mysterious Pusugon's Book" (怪獣 is "monster"), is "Pusugon the Monstrous Book".

**The rest of those help lists (v1.5).** The seven Dai collab skill sets have their own help text, the list synthesis shows under Info when you pick skills to pass on, and each was still one flat line; they now list one skill per line, like the books. "Pile Driver" still named パイルハンマー in nine more help texts, and "Attack Ward+" and "Defense Ward+" still named the ranked Wards in 34 help-list lines; they say "Swelling Strike", "Blunt Ward+" and "Sap Ward+", the names the battle menu and the skill table use.

**Moreheal, and a column that cut at 18 (v1.6).** Twenty-three help lists, ten books and thirteen skill sets, said "Fullheal" where the Japanese names ベホイマ, the spell the battle menu calls "Moreheal"; "Fullheal" is ベホマ, the stronger one. They say "Moreheal" now. And a skill set's Teaches list, under Allocate Skill Points and Library > Skill, cut every action name at 18 characters ("Miracle of the Sta", "Giga Essence Extra"): the function that builds each row gave the name a 21-character buffer, two icons and 18 letters. The buffer is 32 characters now. Five words, found with a live debugger and checked on screen. Eleven names were affected; the longest, "Essence Extractor Dance", fits the column.

**The other screens that cut at 18 (v1.7).** The Teaches column fixed in v1.6 was one of fourteen places in the executable that build an action name with its icons into a 21-character buffer. A debugger hunt through the menus, a battle, the field ability menu and the Library found five more that players see: Library > Abilities cut "Combustive Rending Arts" to "Combustive Rending", and a monster's skill pages, on the status screen and the battle Swap screen, cut "Giga Essence Extractor" and "Miracle of the Stars" the way the Teaches column did. The field ability menu's list, its Info header and the row above Use and Cancel had the same buffer. All five hold 32 characters now, 21 words in all. The battle ability list, its Info window and the action banner never cut. Eight more places with the same buffer were not reached on any screen checked, and are unchanged.

**The quest and hint text, checked against the Japanese.** After the Shiny Sap hint turned out to point at the wrong kind of chest, every quest instruction, hint, tutorial, signpost, shop and NPC guidance line (1,007 of them) was read beside its Japanese with one question: is it true? 125 lines said something the Japanese does not (a place, a direction, a container, a quantity, a condition, or what the player is told to do) and now say what the Japanese says. Three names the dialogue used are now the names the tables use (Δ Slime, Don Mole, Darkiron Bastille), and the two mine puzzle hints say clockwise and counterclockwise.

**The dialogue.** Every NPC line, cutscene line, quest and shop line (5,697 strings) was proofread the same way. 595 spelling, agreement, wrong-word and stray-token slips are fixed, and the names the dialogue spelled two ways are aligned with the game's own tables (Nochoro, Tiko, Lenate, Theresa, Mt. Elpis, Undead Garden and a dozen more). Sixteen lines the 2021 text had garbled are put right from the Japanese, among them the cryogenic-sleep records, the boulder line in the crystal caves, the red and yellow mushrooms that both said pink, and the dying underling who now addresses his two bats instead of claiming to be one.

**The menus.** The last layer: every menu prompt, tutorial pop-up, battle event line, Speed Road and race string, reactor readout, diary entry and network screen (1,614 strings). 122 slips are fixed, and the terms the menus spelled several ways are settled (Present Code, G-Cup, Network Coins, Wi-Fi Square, and SpotPass and StreetPass spelled Nintendo's way everywhere, dialogue included). Ten lines are corrected from the Japanese, among them two Speed Road attacks labelled ranged that are close-range, three that said one slime drops where all of them do, and a garbled diary line about the ship remains.

**The page layout.** Every dialogue page was measured against the same page of the Japanese. In 64 lines the page-break code was wrapped in spaces or glued to the text where the Japanese breaks the line, which left a stray space or a blank line at the top of the next page. A few pages opened with an empty line, 26 pages ran to a third line because a short line had been broken by hand, and one line had two Japanese pages on one. All are back in the Japanese shape, with no wording changed. Five more pages whose English simply had too many words for two lines were shortened, and one prompt that was not a sentence was retranslated. The Bastille's remodeling machine, named nine ways in the 2021 text, has the two names the Japanese gives it; the Snapped family is named by its table names; and the chapter 7 hint, which said the opposite of the Japanese, is retranslated.
